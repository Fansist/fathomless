How to Dive

Controls

  • W A S D↑ ← ↓ → swim
  • SPACE or hold click charge a sonar ping — release to send
  • SHIFT dash (burst of speed, but loud)
  • E or right click drop a decoy buoy
  • P / ESC pause   M mute   F fullscreen
  • Gamepad: stick + A (hold), B dash, X decoy

The Deep

  • You cannot see. Pings paint the cave in light, then fade. Walls you have mapped stay faintly drawn.
  • Loud pings travel far — and wake the hunters. A quick tap is a whisper.
  • Orange echoes are creatures. They hear, they don't see. Drifting slowly is silent; sprinting is not.
  • Ghost images show where something was, not where it is.
  • Gold pearls feed your energy and score. Decoys lure creatures away.
  • Reach the glowing gate. Twelve depths to the Heart.
